Get the skill

Give your agents the skills they need.

Skills don’t work if your setup rejects them. Skillproof finds what you need and fits it into your setup — seamlessly, with your permission.

Nothing to install — paste one short prompt into Claude (browser or app) and that chat finds the right skill for your pain point — from our catalog or the wider web — and fits it into your setup, with your permission.

Download the catalog

For Claude Code. Downloads seven files to disk so you can read them — nothing is piped into a shell, and it never edits your setup without asking first, per change.

for f in SKILL.md references/consent.md references/conflict-patterns.md references/install-paths.md references/tiers.md references/finding.md references/security.md; do curl -fsSL --create-dirs https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lucascashwell3-ai/Skillproof/main/skills/skillproof/$f -o ~/.claude/skills/skillproof/$f; done
