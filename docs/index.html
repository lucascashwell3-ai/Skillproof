Honesty note: keyword matching over a small curated set — not semantic search. Results come in two tiers: graded (tested, with receipts) and scouted (found + triaged, explicitly ungraded). No match ≠ no tool exists — take the scout with you and search the live ecosystem.

The graded index

Every grade recomputes from five tested dimensions — triggering, effectiveness (double-weighted), docs & install, maintenance, safety — and links to the worksheet that produced it. Grades reflect the exact commit tested, on the date shown. We don't guess, and we never publish a grade we didn't run.

Scouted, not yet graded

Resources the scout found and triaged — repo verified real, license and last-push checked, safety skimmed — but never installed, never probed. They carry no grade by design; the honesty gate rejects any that try. Grading queue runs top-down.

What a grade is — and isn't. A Skillproof grade is the documented result of one grading run: real install, real trigger probes in fresh sessions, the skill's headline job executed in a sandboxed container, every line of its source read. It is not an endorsement, not a popularity score, and it can go stale — grades older than 90 days are flagged. Disagree with one? Open an issue; we re-run the protocol and publish the result either way.
