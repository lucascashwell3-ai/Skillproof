# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI power users and developers who use Claude (Code/Desktop) daily and are drowning in
untested community skills, libraries, and "10 tools" listicles. They arrive with a pain
point ("my frontend output looks generic", "long sessions degrade", "my commits are a mess")
and want a trustworthy answer fast — installed, tested, receipted — not another directory.
They are mid-task when they arrive; the site is a tool they use, not a page they admire.

## Product Purpose

Skillproof is **the rating agency for Claude skills**: every listed skill was actually
installed, probed in real headless sessions, and graded A–F against a published rubric,
each grade linking to its worksheet (receipts). The pain-point matcher turns a described
problem into a vetted stack. The scout (skill + MCP server + paste-in prompt) extends the
same discipline to the ungraded ecosystem: it finds candidate skills/libraries/resources
and triages them honestly — provenance, license, safety red flags, freshness — always
labeled "scouted, ungraded", never a fabricated grade. Success = a user leaves with
something installed that works, and never feels lied to.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, honest, understated. "Grades first, honesty always." Part of the warm gold-on-dark
`-proof` family (with MODELproof / DATproof): Fraunces display + Inter text, proof-seal mark,
capsule chrome, one satin-gold accent. Restraint register — this is a daily-use tool, not a
showcase piece.

## Anti-references

- Skill directories that count everything and vet nothing (the rejected lane — 6+ incumbents).
- Affiliate-flavored "best AI tools" listicles; hype language; unverifiable claims.
- SaaS-gradient hero-metric templates; glassmorphism-by-default; cosmic-purple AI slop.

## Design Principles

1. **The receipts ARE the interface.** Every grade shown links to the worksheet that produced
   it; every scouted item states plainly that it is ungraded.
2. **Honesty over coverage.** "We haven't graded one yet" is a first-class result state with
   a useful next step (the scout), never a dead end or a bluffed answer.
3. **Practice what we grade.** The site itself must pass the standards the rubric demands of
   skills: fires fast, does its one job, nothing hidden.
4. **Restraint.** One accent, quiet motion, capsule chrome; the data carries the drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast targets on dark ground; full keyboard operability for the matcher (chips,
input, results); `prefers-reduced-motion` honored (starfield and transitions disabled);
aria-live on match results; no information carried by color alone (grades always paired
with letter + score).
